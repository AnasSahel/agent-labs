# ISO 42001

AI management system (AIMS). Same structure as ISO 27001, so clauses 4 to 10 map one to one; its Annex A (38 controls, A.2 to A.10) is mostly about AI and lands in Extras. Cited at objective level (`A.6`); single controls only after checking, else "clause to confirm". The natural evidence for the AI Act.

## Mapping

One row per clause. **Fit** `full`: the ISO 27001 refs meet the clause. `partial`: they help, and the rest is in Extras.

## Extras

Duties the pivot does not carry. **gap-analysis** checks them on their own; a risk touches one when its feared event would trigger it.

| Clause | Duty | Why ISO 27001 misses it |
|---|---|---|
| Cl. 6.1 | AI system impact assessment (Cl. 6.1.4) on individuals and society, not only on the organization | ISO 27001 risks are to information |
| Cl. 8 | Run the impact assessment on each AI system and at each significant change | AI-specific |
| A.2 | AI policy aligned with the other policies, reviewed | AI-specific |
| A.4 | Document the resources each AI system needs | AI-specific |
| A.5 | Document the impact assessment process and its results | AI-specific |
| A.6 | Responsible development objectives, verification and validation of models | AI-specific |
| A.7 | Data quality, provenance and preparation for training and testing | Data quality, not security |
| A.8 | User documentation, incident reporting channel for users, communication of incidents | AI-specific |
| A.9 | Objectives and processes for responsible use, within intended use | AI-specific |

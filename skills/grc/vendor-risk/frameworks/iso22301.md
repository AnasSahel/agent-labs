# ISO 22301

Business continuity management system (BCMS). Same structure as ISO 27001 (clauses 4 to 10), so the management system maps one to one; the continuity work itself (clause 8) goes well beyond ISO 27001's A.5.29 and A.5.30.

## Mapping

One row per clause. **Fit** `full`: the ISO 27001 refs meet the clause. `partial`: they help, and the rest is in Extras.

| Clause | Requirement | ISO 27001 | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cl. 4 | Context, interested parties, legal requirements, scope of the BCMS | 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4 | full |
| Cl. 5 | Leadership, business continuity policy, roles | 5.1, 5.2, 5.3 | full |
| Cl. 6 | Planning: risks to the BCMS, objectives, changes | 6.2, 6.3 | full |
| Cl. 7 | Support: resources, competence, awareness, communication, documents | 7.2, 7.3, 7.4, 7.5 | full |
| Cl. 8.2 | Business impact analysis and risk assessment | 6.1.2, A.5.30 | partial |
| Cl. 8.3 | Business continuity strategies and solutions | A.5.29, A.5.30, A.8.14 | partial |
| Cl. 8.4 | Plans and procedures: response structure, warning and communication, recovery | A.5.24, A.5.26, A.5.29, A.5.30 | partial |
| Cl. 8.5 | Exercise programme | A.5.30 | partial |
| Cl. 8.6 | Evaluate business continuity documentation and capabilities | 9.1, A.5.30 | full |
| Cl. 9 | Performance evaluation: monitoring, internal audit, management review | 9.1, 9.2, 9.3 | full |
| Cl. 10 | Improvement: nonconformity, corrective action, continual improvement | 10.1, 10.2 | full |

## Extras

Duties the pivot does not carry. **gap-analysis** checks them on their own; a risk touches one when its feared event would trigger it.

| Clause | Duty | Why ISO 27001 misses it |
|---|---|---|
| Cl. 8.2 | BIA for every product and service: prioritized activities, maximum tolerable period of disruption, RTO, RPO, minimum resources | ISO 27001 covers ICT readiness, not the business impact |
| Cl. 8.3 | Strategies for people, facilities, suppliers and information, not only ICT | ISO 27001 stops at ICT |
| Cl. 8.4 | Warn and communicate with interested parties and media during a disruption | Crisis communication |
| Cl. 8.5 | Exercise regularly against objectives, with post-exercise reports | ISO 27001 asks to test ICT continuity, not run exercises |

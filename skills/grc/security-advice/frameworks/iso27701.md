# ISO 27701

Extends an ISO 27001 ISMS into a privacy information management system (PIMS), for PII controllers (Annex A) and processors (Annex B). The best bridge to GDPR: when both are in scope, cite this file for the privacy side.

## Mapping

One row per clause. **Fit** `full`: the ISO 27001 refs meet the clause. `partial`: they help, and the rest is in Extras.

| Clause | Requirement | ISO 27001 | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cl. 5 | PIMS requirements on top of ISO 27001 clauses 4 to 10 (role as controller or processor, privacy in risk assessment) | 4.1, 4.3, 6.1.2, 6.1.3 | full |
| Cl. 6 | Privacy guidance on the ISO 27002 controls | A.5.34 | full |
| A.7.2 | Conditions for collection and processing: purpose, lawful basis, consent, impact assessment, processor contracts, records | 6.1.2, A.5.9, A.5.20, A.5.34 | partial |
| A.7.3 | Obligations to PII principals: information, consent withdrawal, access, correction, erasure | A.5.34 | partial |
| A.7.4 | Privacy by design and by default: minimisation, accuracy, retention, de-identification, deletion | A.5.33, A.8.10, A.8.11 | partial |
| A.7.5 | PII sharing, transfer and disclosure, with records | A.5.14, A.5.20 | partial |
| B.8.2 | Processor: contract, processing only on instructions, records | A.5.20, A.6.2 | partial |
| B.8.3 | Processor: help the controller meet its obligations to PII principals | A.5.34 | partial |

## Extras

Duties the pivot does not carry. **gap-analysis** checks them on their own; a risk touches one when its feared event would trigger it.

| Clause | Duty | Why ISO 27001 misses it |
|---|---|---|
| A.7.2 | Identify the purpose and lawful basis of each processing, record consent, run a privacy impact assessment | Privacy, not security |
| A.7.3 | Answer PII principals' requests (access, correction, erasure, objection) | Not a security topic |
| A.7.4 | Collect only what the purpose needs, keep it accurate, set retention per purpose | ISO 27001 secures data; it doesn't limit it |
| A.7.5 | Record transfers between jurisdictions and disclosures to third parties | A transfer register |
| B.8.2 | Tell the controller when an instruction infringes the law | A processor duty |
| B.8.3 | Give the controller what it needs to answer PII principals | A processor duty |
| B.8.5 | Tell the customer before changing subprocessors, so it can object | A processor duty |

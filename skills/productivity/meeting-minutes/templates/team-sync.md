# Team sync

**Signals:** recurring, short, "yesterday / today", blockers, several people each giving an update.

## Body
- `## Updates`: one line per person or workstream: done, doing next.
- `## Blockers`: what is stuck, who can unblock, by when.

**Rules:** keep it to a page. A blocker without an unblocker is an open question. Skip `Open questions` when empty.

# Audit or compliance meeting

**Signals:** an auditor, a framework (ISO 27001, NIS2, DORA, GDPR, SOC 2), controls, findings, evidence, non-conformities.

## Body
- `## Scope`: framework, controls or processes covered.
- `## Findings`: table `# | Finding | Control or clause | Severity | Owner`. Severity as the auditor stated it.
- `## Evidence`: provided, requested (with owner and date), refused.
- `## Auditor's next steps`: report date, follow-up visit.

**Rules:** cite a control or clause only when the notes name it; otherwise `clause to confirm`, never from memory. Findings are not decisions. Evidence requests are actions. With `grc/`, point to `audit-evidence` for filing proof and `risk-register` for accepted risks.

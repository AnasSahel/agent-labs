# Review or retro

**Signals:** sprint review, post-mortem, lessons learned, "what went well", "what to improve".

## Body
- `## What worked`
- `## What didn't`
- `## Causes`: only if the notes discuss why.
- `## Improvements`: each becomes an action with an owner.

**Rules:** blameless: name processes and systems, not people, unless the notes credit someone's win. Every improvement is an action or it is dropped.

# Steering committee

**Signals:** a project or programme, status colors, budget, milestones, risks, escalations, sponsor present.

## Body
- `## Status`: overall (on track, at risk, late) in one line, then per workstream: status, one line why.
- `## Milestones and budget`: table `Milestone | Planned | Forecast | Status`, spend vs budget when given.
- `## Risks and issues`: table `Risk or issue | Impact | Owner | Mitigation`.
- `## Escalations`: what the committee is asked to decide, and the answer.

**Rules:** the verdict (on track, at risk, late) comes from the notes, never your guess. Each escalation ends in a decision or an open question. With `grc/`, risks may be register ids.

# Decision or board meeting

**Signals:** "resolution", "approved", "voted", quorum, a chair, formal agenda items, legal or governance weight.

## Body
- `## Quorum`: members present against required, when known.
- `## Agenda items`: per item: the question, the discussion in two lines, the outcome.
- `## Resolutions`: numbered, each as `Resolved: <text>`, with vote (`for / against / abstain`) and who proposed it.

**Rules:** write only what the notes support for votes and quorum; otherwise ask, and remove the line if skipped. Mark conflicts of interest declared. Decisions here are formal: the Decisions table repeats the resolutions, with the vote in `Context`. Minutes stay `draft` until the chair approves.

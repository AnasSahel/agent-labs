# Project kickoff

**Signals:** a project start, "goals", "scope", "who does what", first meeting of a team.

## Body
- `## Goal and success`: why the project exists, how success is measured.
- `## Scope`: in, out.
- `## Roles`: table `Who | Role | Responsibility`.
- `## Milestones`: table `Milestone | Date`.
- `## Risks and assumptions`: short list.
- `## Ways of working`: rituals, tools, channels.

**Rules:** ask for a role or date nobody stated; remove it if skipped. Out of scope is listed only if said.

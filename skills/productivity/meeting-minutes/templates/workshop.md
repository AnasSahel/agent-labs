# Workshop or brainstorm

**Signals:** post-its, ideas, voting, themes, "how might we", facilitation.

## Body
- `## Question`: what the workshop worked on.
- `## Ideas by theme`: group ideas under 3 to 6 themes, a line each.
- `## Shortlist`: the ideas kept, with why and the vote count if any.
- `## Parked`: ideas left out for later.

**Rules:** group, do not rank, unless there was a vote. Ideas are not decisions: only the shortlist choice counts as one. Next steps are actions.

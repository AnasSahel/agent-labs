# Client or vendor call

**Signals:** an external party, requests, pricing, scope, deadlines, "they asked", "we promised".

## Body
- `## Their requests`: what the other side asked for.
- `## Our commitments`: what we promised, with owner and date.
- `## Their commitments`: what they promised, with their contact and date.
- `## Commercial points`: price, scope, contract, only if raised.

**Rules:** keep commitments on both sides separate and never soften one into a wish. Write it so it could be sent to the other party: no internal opinions. Their actions go in the Actions table with the owner's company in brackets.

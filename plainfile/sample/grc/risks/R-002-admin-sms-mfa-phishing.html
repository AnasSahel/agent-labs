Risk register · R-002 · assessed 2026-10-30

A phishing gang could take payments offline and steal customer data by phishing an IT admin's login, despite SMS codes

Owner CTOStatus open Due 2026-11-30 (entry point) · 2026-12-31 (all) Frameworks ISO 27001 · NIS2 · DORA · GDPR

Verdict

Today the risk is high · G4·V3, above the appetite (medium at most). SMS codes do not stop phishing: a fake Microsoft login page captures the password and the code and replays them within seconds. Admins sign in to AWS through Entra ID single sign-on, so one phished login opens production.

With the five controls below the risk drops to medium · G4·V1, within appetite. The worst case stays critical; the controls make it unlikely.

# A note can't be sent to someone else

## Problem

People who take notes for a team have no way to pass one on: they copy the text by hand into an email. It takes time and the copy drifts from the note.

## Why now

Two teams started using the app for meeting follow-ups this month.

## Success looks like

Someone can send a note to a colleague's email address from the app, and later see who each note was sent to.

## Out of scope

Sending to several people at once.

## Constraints

Mail goes through the company's SMTP relay.

## Open questions

How long should the record of who received a note be kept?

## Evidence

"I paste my notes into Outlook ten times a day."
